Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

The short version: this website does not use cookies. At all.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser to recognize you between page views or visits. They can be useful (keeping you logged in) or invasive (tracking you across the web for advertising).

What this site does

Nothing. This site sets no first-party cookies and loads nothing that could set third-party cookies: no analytics, no advertising pixels, no embedded videos, no social media buttons, no external fonts or scripts. It also uses no equivalent technologies such as localStorage-based tracking or fingerprinting.

This is why you do not see a cookie consent banner here: under the GDPR and the ePrivacy rules, consent banners are required for sites that store or read non-essential information on your device. This site stores nothing, so there is nothing to consent to.

If this ever changes

If we ever add functionality that requires cookies (for example a newsletter with login), this policy will be updated first, and where consent is legally required, we will ask for it before setting anything.

For everything else this site does and does not process, see the privacy policy.
