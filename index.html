An experiment in public

What if a company had zero employees — and still ran itself?

The Zero Employee Company is a real business operated end-to-end by AI agents. No staff. No freelancers. Just software doing the work — and one human documenting everything that happens along the way.

The Idea

AI can now write, sell, support, analyze, and decide. So here's the question this project exists to answer: can an entire company — product, marketing, operations, customer service — run without a single employee?

Principle 01

Radically lean

No payroll, no office, no meetings. Every function of the business is handled by an AI agent or an automation — or it doesn't happen.

Principle 02

Built in public

Every experiment, tool choice, cost, failure, and win gets documented here. The transparency is the point.

Principle 03

Human on the loop

One founder sets direction and reviews outcomes. The AI does the work. The interesting part is finding where that line actually sits.

How It Works

The company is a stack of AI agents wired together, each owning a job a person would normally do.

Ops

Operations agents

Handle scheduling, invoicing, reporting, and the day-to-day admin that usually eats a founder's week.

Growth

Marketing agents

Write content, run campaigns, and analyze what's working — then adjust without being asked.

Support

Customer agents

Answer questions, resolve issues, and escalate to the human only when something genuinely needs judgment.

Build

Product agents

Ship and maintain the product itself — including this website, which was built and deployed by an AI agent.

About

The Zero Employee Company is run by Giovanni Brees — or more accurately, it's run by AI, and Giovanni watches, steers, and writes about it.

This project isn't a prediction that jobs will disappear. It's a live experiment to find out what's actually possible today, what breaks, what still needs a human, and what a one-person company can realistically achieve with the current generation of AI tools.
